# Gantry CRM: Instagram launch plan

This replaces the Gemini plan. Use `docs/GEMINI-KIT.md` for the video and caption prompts; use this
file for what to do and in what order.

## The decisions

| Question                | Decision                                                                                     | Why                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Which trade?            | **HVAC and plumbing service companies, 3 to 10 people**                                      | They run on phone calls, urgent jobs, a schedule and technicians in vans. That is exactly what Gantry does best and what rivals do worst for the price. |
| Which plan do we lead?  | **Gantry Complete** ($500 setup + $70/mo, 10 people). Fallback: **Gantry Jobs** ($300 + $45) | These firms also quote replacements, so Complete fits. Jobs is the cheaper way in for anyone who balks at the price.                                    |
| What does Instagram do? | **A credibility page, not the main lead engine**                                             | A new faceless account gets almost no organic reach in month one. Its job is to make a contractor you contact think "these people are real".            |
| How do leads arrive?    | **Direct messages to HVAC and plumbing owners, backed by real screen demos**                 | Direct contact is the only thing that reliably produces conversations in the first 30 days.                                                             |
| What is the offer?      | **A free 15-minute walkthrough using their own jobs**                                        | There is no free trial. A walkthrough costs you 15 minutes and sells far better than a link.                                                            |
| Who does walkthroughs?  | **You, on a video call, with your face off if you like (screen only)**                       | The call is where it closes. Faceless on the page, human on the call.                                                                                   |
| What kind of video?     | **Real screen recordings with voiceover. AI footage only as a 2 to 3 second opener**         | Contractors trust a real app. Heavy AI footage reads as fake and hurts trust.                                                                           |
| How often?              | **4 Reels a week, plus 30 to 45 minutes a day of messaging**                                 | Consistency matters more than volume. Messaging is where the leads come from.                                                                           |

## 2. What the account says (positioning)

One sentence: **"Gantry is the simple CRM for HVAC and plumbing crews. It keeps the call, the job, the
schedule and the quote in one place, so nothing falls through the cracks."**

Three promises, all true today:

1. Every call gets a due time and a countdown, so nothing urgent sits.
2. Your technician sees their day on a phone, starts and finishes the visit, and captures a signature.
3. Quote and send the PDF before you leave the driveway.

Never claim: free trial, invoicing or payments, automatic texting, an app-store app, guaranteed
results, or any statistic you cannot source.

## 4. The messaging engine (where leads actually come from)

**Targets**: HVAC and plumbing owners with 2 to 10 people in a city or two, found through Instagram
search on `#hvac`, `#plumber`, `#hvaclife` plus a city name, and Google Maps (look for companies with
a website that is basic or no scheduling tool). Make a simple sheet: business, owner, city, size,
date messaged, reply, next step.

**Daily routine** (30 to 45 minutes): follow 20 targets, like and leave a useful comment on 10 posts,
and send 10 to 15 direct messages. Stay under these numbers: Instagram limits new accounts that
message too fast.

### Message 1 (cold, short, no pitch)

> Hey [name], saw your [specific job or truck] in [city]. Quick question: how do you handle quotes and
> scheduling when it gets busy? Building something for crews like yours and I'd like to hear how you
> do it.

### Message 2 (if they reply)

> Thanks, that makes sense. A lot of crews tell me the same thing: [the pain they described]. I made
> a short video on exactly that: [link to the matching Reel]. If it looks useful I can show you it on
> your own jobs in 15 minutes. No pitch, no card.

### Message 3 (book the call)

> How about [two specific times]? I'll share my screen, you give me a typical week, and I'll show how
> it would look for you.

### If they ignore you

Wait 3 days, send one follow-up ("No worries if it's not a fit. Want me to send the 60-second
version?"), then stop. Do not message a third time.

### Comments (on their posts, not pitches)

- "That's a clean install. What size system?"
- "Good tip. Same thing we hear from crews all the time."
- Never mention Gantry in a comment unless they ask.

### If someone comments or DMs "DEMO" on your Reels

> Thanks! 15 minutes, screen share, I'll use your real jobs. What's your trade, how many people, and
> what's the biggest headache right now?

## 5. The walkthrough call (15 minutes, you close it here)

1. **(2 min) Their week.** "Walk me through a normal day. Where does a call go? How does the tech
   know where to be? How do you send quotes?" Write down the three pains.
2. **(8 min) Show only those three things** in the demo business, using their words. Do not tour
   every feature.
3. **(3 min) Price, plainly:**
   > Gantry Complete is $500 to set up and $70 a month, with 10 people included. If you only need
   > jobs and scheduling, Gantry Jobs is $300 and $45 a month. Extra people are $5 a month. You can
   > cancel any time; you keep it until the end of the paid month, and the setup fee isn't refunded.
4. **(2 min) The next step.** Send them `/buy?plan=complete` (once Stripe is live), or tell them you
   will email an invoice and their licence key the same day.

### Objections

| They say                      | You say                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "I'll look at it later."      | "No problem. What would need to be true for this to be a clear yes? I'll send a quick video of that part."             |
| "Too expensive."              | "Fair. A missed urgent call or a late quote is worth more than $70. Want to start with Jobs at $45?"                   |
| "I use paper / a whiteboard." | "That works until two people need it at once. Want me to show you how to keep the whiteboard and still log the calls?" |
| "I have a CRM already."       | "What do you like and not like about it? If it does the job, stay put."                                                |
| "What if you disappear?"      | "Your data exports to Excel or CSV any time, so you are never stuck."                                                  |
| "Is it secure?"               | "Each business is kept completely separate, and you decide who sees what with roles."                                  |

Never promise features that are not built (invoicing, payments, texting, an app-store app). If they
ask, say it is not there today and write it on your list.

## 7. What to measure

Count these every Friday in a simple sheet.

| Number                         | Why it matters                               |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| Messages sent                  | The input you control                        |
| Replies (and reply rate)       | Tells you if the opener works (aim for 15%+) |
| Walkthroughs booked and held   | The real lead number                         |
| Customers                      | The only number that pays                    |
| Saves and shares per Reel      | Which topics to repeat                       |
| Link clicks and profile visits | Whether the page builds credibility          |

Followers are not a goal. Decide at day 30 using this rule: if messages are going out but replies are
below 10%, change the opener; if replies are fine but few walkthroughs happen, change the offer; if
walkthroughs happen but nobody buys, change the price framing or the demo.

`#plumberlife` `#hvactech` `#contractorlife` `#tradesman` `#smallbusiness` `#businesstips`

Compliance, every post:

- Label AI footage with Instagram's AI label. Say when a voice is AI.
- Demo data only on screen. No real customer names, phone numbers or addresses.
- No made-up statistics, customers or testimonials. A real quote from a real customer is welcome once
  you have one and their permission in writing.
- Licensed music only.
- Respect Instagram's messaging limits and never message the same person more than twice.
- Do not run ads until the buy flow works end to end.
